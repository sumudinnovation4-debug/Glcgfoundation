Questions about giving

How do I know my gift reaches beneficiaries?

Designated gifts are used for the programme you name. Contact the secretariat to ask for updates on the project you supported.

Can I sponsor a specific child or borehole?

Yes. Tell us which you would like to support and we will share progress updates with you.

How can churches or companies partner with GLCGF?

We welcome partnerships with corporate social responsibility teams, foundations, religious bodies and volunteers. Reach us through the contact page.

Will I receive a receipt?

Email your transfer confirmation to info@glcgfoundation.org and we will respond with an acknowledgement.
